# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

High-net-worth individuals, property investors, and families seeking premium residential and commercial real estate. They value quality, location, and a trusted developer track record.

## Product Purpose

To serve as the digital flagship for SVI Infra Solutions, showcasing their 15+ years of experience and premium property portfolio. It exists to drive high-quality leads, provide an immersive property exploration experience (via 3D and animations), and establish absolute brand trust.

## Brand Personality

Premium, immersive, established, architectural.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI slop" aesthetics (clichéd card grids, floating ghost-cards with wide shadows, gradient text).
- Boring, template-driven corporate real estate sites.
- Flat or uninspired layouts that fail to convey the physical reality of the properties.

## Design Principles

- **Immersive Reality:** Use cinematic 3D graphics and high-performance motion to make the properties feel tangible before they are built.
- **Uncompromising Polish:** Premium execution at every touchpoint—refined typography, intentional spacing, and flawless responsive behavior.
- **Expert Confidence:** The design should feel established and authoritative, reflecting 15+ years of successful deliveries without shouting.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliance baseline.
- Reduced motion alternatives for all heavy 3D and cinematic animations (`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`).
- High contrast typography that remains legible over immersive backgrounds.
