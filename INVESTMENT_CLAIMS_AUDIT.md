# SVI Infra Solutions — Investment & ROI Claims Audit

## 1. Overview

In accordance with Priority 3 guidelines:

> "If a percentage has no reliable source or methodology: DO NOT present it as an expected outcome. Convert it into clearly labeled illustrative content... If the number is historical: State source, geographic area, time period, methodology. If it cannot be supported: REMOVE THE NUMBER."

Every explicit numeric percentage claim (`15-20%`, `18-25%`, `2.5x to 3x`, etc.) across the entire site was audited.
