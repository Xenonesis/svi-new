#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: High AI slop detected. The hero section relies heavily on generic "premium AI" defaults: glowing drop shadows (`shadow-[0_0_40px_rgba(212,175,55,0.3)]`), gradient text (`text-gradient-gold`), and an `aurora-bg`. The floating "15+ Years" stat card uses a typical "ghost card" (`border-white/10 bg-black/20 backdrop-blur-xl`) which makes the site feel like a generic SaaS template rather than a multi-million dollar real estate developer.

**Deterministic scan**: Detector returned clean (0 findings), meaning the slop is stylistic rather than structural.

#### Overall Impression

The foundation is solid and the layout is functional, but the aesthetic execution feels cheap and templated. The single biggest opportunity is replacing the 2D crossfade slider and glowing elements with actual WebGL/Three.js architecture to match the user's request for "3D graphics."

#### What's Working

1. **Layout Asymmetry**: Left-aligned text with a right-aligned stat card breaks the boring center-aligned mold.
2. **Typography Scale**: The title sizing (up to `text-8xl`) gives the right dramatic scale for luxury real estate.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P0] Aesthetic**: Missing 3D Graphics
  - **Why it matters**: The user explicitly requested 3D graphics for a premium feel, but the current hero uses a standard 2D image crossfade.
  - **Fix**: Implement a Three.js canvas in the background or for the main visual element.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable craft` or `$impeccable overdrive` (to rewrite with Three.js).

- **[P1] Aesthetic**: "AI Slop" Effects
  - **Why it matters**: Gradient text, shimmer effects, glowing shadows, and aurora backgrounds make the site look like an AI wrapper, destroying trust for high-end real estate buyers.
  - **Fix**: Switch to solid, confident colors (e.g., solid gold or white text), remove drop-shadows on buttons, and use a stark, architectural background.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable polish` (to strip out the slop).

- **[P2] Component**: Generic Ghost Cards
  - **Why it matters**: The floating stat card uses a tired glassmorphism effect. Premium design uses solid geometry or stark contrast, not blurry transparency.
  - **Fix**: Change the stat card to a solid background with a sharp 1px border.
  - **Suggested command**: `$impeccable harden`.

#### Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User/Investor)**: The glowing buttons and aurora background signal "tech startup" rather than "established real estate developer," potentially causing trust issues for high-net-worth investments.
**Jordan (First-Timer)**: The hero text gradient might be hard to read on certain displays due to the complex background image + aurora mix.
